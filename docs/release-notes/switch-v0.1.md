# Metroid: Zero Mission NX 0.1 — experimental Switch release

- Native Switch gameplay and audio with saved volume controls.
- R3 Popup, Side by Side, and clockwise Flip Grip layouts.
- Touch map, status, save-state, achievement, and settings tabs.
- Five save-state slots and separate normal in-game saves.
- Softcore RetroAchievements with cached definitions, queued sync, and badge notifications.
- Includes crash and connection diagnostics; no ROM included.

NRO author: JJTapia_XX. This Switch adaptation derives from tinaut1986/mzm and metroidret/mzm; all upstream and component credits and license notices are retained.
